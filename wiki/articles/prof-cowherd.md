The cowherd looks after the cows of the village's pasture. Its workstation is the milk churn ([[item:milk_churn]]), beside the pasture. It is not a vanilla trade.

### Where cowherds come from
In the default world every village on grass with 10 or more villagers has one, with a pasture beside its churn. The pasture starts with 2 to 4 cows. A builder may put up a pasture in a grassy village that has none; it starts empty.

### The working day
During working hours the cowherd:

- **Milks** every adult cow once a day, and bottles the milk at the churn: 1 bucket of milk and 3 glass bottles make 3 milk bottles, and the bucket comes back.
- **Feeds** wheat to hungry adults while the herd is at or under its limit (one cow for every 8 cells of pasture).
- **Culls** adults when the herd is over the limit, never below 2 adults. Each yields raw beef and leather, kept to sell.
- **Stocks the pasture** if it holds fewer than 2 cows, by leading a wild cow home from within 96 blocks.
- **Cooks steak** from raw beef in a real furnace with fuel it has bought, keeping 6 raw beef to sell and cooking the rest until it holds 24 steaks.

Calves take 3 days to grow up and give no milk. See [[page:animal-husbandry]].

### Where it gets its materials
Wheat comes from the farmers. It buys empty glass bottles back from the village whenever it has fewer than 9, up to 30, from the villagers who drank the milk or the [[villager:glassmaker]] who makes new ones. Fuel for the furnace is bought as well.

### What it sells and buys
Milk bottles, raw beef, leather and steak. It keeps 3 milk bottles for the [[villager:baker]]. It buys wheat and empty bottles from you. It never sells its bucket.

### Starting kit
A bucket and 30 glass bottles, once per villager. It never starts with milk, beef, leather or steak. Nothing is restocked.
