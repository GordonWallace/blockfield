Cows graze in open grassland in groups of four to eight, moving slowly and pausing often. They are harmless. A cow you hit runs away for a few seconds. They are one of the commonest animals on plains, and the "cow country" zones described in [[page:mob-spawning]] can hold several herds together.

### Feeding and breeding

Right click a cow holding [[item:wheat_item]] to feed it. Cows within 10 blocks of a player holding wheat follow them, which is the easy way to lead one into a pasture. A fed cow is willing for one game day. Two willing cows close together walk to each other, and a calf appears. Calves are half size, drop nothing, give no milk and take three game days to become adults. The parents rest a day before they will breed again. No breeding happens while 24 or more cows are within 16 blocks. See [[page:animal-husbandry]].

### Milk

Right click an adult cow with an empty bucket to get a bucket of milk. Each cow can be milked once per game day; trying again says "This cow was milked today". Calves cannot be milked. In creative mode you keep the bucket. Milk can be drunk as food, poured into glass bottles with a bucket (three bottles from one bucket) and used by bakers for cakes.

### In villages

On grass villages with ten or more villagers, a [[villager:cowherd]] works at a **milk churn** beside a **pasture**, an 11 by 9 fenced field with a gate, hay bales and a water trough. The pasture starts with two to four cows and holds up to six. The cowherd:

- milks every adult cow once a day, bottling the milk;
- feeds the herd wheat so that it stays near the limit, and keeps a piece of wheat as a lure;
- culls adults when the pasture is over its limit, always keeping at least two adults, and sells the beef and leather;
- leads a wild cow home from up to 96 blocks when the pasture holds fewer than two;
- cooks surplus beef into steak in a village furnace.

Builders can build a pasture in a grass village that has none. The [[villager:leatherworker]] buys the leather that cows give. A cow that wanders into a pasture joins it.
