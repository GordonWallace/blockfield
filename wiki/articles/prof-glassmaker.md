The glassmaker digs sand and turns it into glass, panes and bottles. Its workstation is the glass kiln ([[item:glass_kiln]]). It is not a vanilla trade.

### Where glassmakers come from
A newly generated village with natural sand within 64 blocks and at least 4 villagers gets a glassmaker 80 percent of the time, with a kiln and a furnace placed by the village's plan. Where there is sand but no glassmaker, a [[villager:builder]] may build a glassworks.

### The working day
- **Digs sand.** It takes the top layer of natural sand open to the sky within 64 blocks of the village or its kiln: beaches, riverbanks, desert. It never digs a placed block, sand under or beside water, or anything on the village's buildings, plots, roads, farms or built structures. It digs one layer only, so a dug spot is never dug deeper, and at most a stack (64) a day. With a shovel a block takes 0.8 seconds, by hand 2.
- **Buys sand** from anyone with spare, and from you.
- **Smelts** its sand into glass in a village furnace, the one beside its kiln first, in loads of 8 to 20 sand, with fuel it holds or buys: coal or charcoal first, then logs and planks.
- **Crafts** glass into bottles (3 glass make 3 bottles) and panes (6 glass make 16 panes) at its kiln: enough bottles for the [[villager:cowherd]]'s empties, panes for the [[villager:builder|builders]]' current and next structures, and the rest stays glass.

It stops making glass when it holds 48.

### What it sells
Glass, glass panes and glass bottles, and from the second level larger batches. It buys sand from you. It keeps back its sand and fuel.

### Starting kit
A founding glassmaker starts with a stone shovel. It never starts with glass, panes or bottles: every glass item in the village comes from sand that was dug or bought and smelted with real fuel. Nothing is restocked.

Without a glassmaker, a village's builders leave their windows open until panes turn up.
