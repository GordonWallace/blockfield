The furniture maker makes beds, chests and furnaces. Its workstation is the [[item:carpentry_bench]], placed on the plaza. It is not a vanilla trade.

### Where furniture makers come from
A village generated with this version that has both a [[villager:shepherd]] and a [[villager:forester]] gets one 80 percent of the time. Older villages do not gain one.

### The working day
At its bench it makes:

- **Beds.** One bed is 3 wool of any colour and 3 planks of any wood. It makes them while it holds fewer than 6, and saws logs into planks first if it is short.
- **Chests.** A villager whose inventory is full and whose house has no chest orders one. The furniture maker makes a chest from 8 planks, carries it to the house and puts it down on the spot the villager chose, taking 1 emerald from the villager's purse. These deliveries come before bed sales.
- **Furnaces.** From 8 cobblestone each. It always keeps one in stock, whatever the demand. A [[villager:toolsmith]] with no furnace in the village comes to buy it.

### Where it gets its materials
It buys wool from the [[villager:shepherd]], planks and logs from the [[villager:forester]] and cobblestone from the [[villager:miner]], walking to them and paying at their offers like anyone else.

### What it sells
Beds, chests and furnaces. The [[villager:builder|builders]] are its customers: it walks to a builder that wants beds (what its structure still needs, plus up to 2 in hand for the next house) and sells at its own offer, paid from the builder's purse. Builders short of beds also come to it. It buys wool, planks and logs, and at higher levels cobblestone and more kinds of wood, from you. It is the only villager that sells beds.

### Starting kit
Two beds, the wool and planks for one more, and a furnace. A founding furniture maker is given none of what it buys. No beds are restocked: every bed exists because wool and planks were turned into it.
