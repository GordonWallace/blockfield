The leatherworker trades in leather. Its workstation is the [[item:cauldron]]. It does nothing of its own beyond visiting its cauldron and shopping.

### Where it gets its leather
Leather is never created for it. When it holds fewer than 12, the leatherworker walks to the [[villager:cowherd]] or the [[villager:butcher]], or to anyone selling spare leather, and buys it an offer at a time until it holds about 18. It also buys leather, raw beef, flint and string from you.

### What it sells
Leather in larger batches as it levels up, and books at the third level. It buys rotten flesh from you.

The [[villager:stable_hand]] and builders, who need leather for saddles, leads and tack racks, buy it from whichever villagers sell it, and the leatherworker is one of them.

### Restock
None. The daily restock does not touch the leatherworker, so its leather is only what it has bought or what you sold it.
