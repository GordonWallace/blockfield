---
title: Animal husbandry
category: Mobs
---
Pigs, cows, sheep, chickens and horses can all be fed, bred and kept. Villagers do the same with fenced pens, and wild animals breed on their own too. The rules are nearly the same for every kind.

## Feeding and breeding

Feeding is a right click with the right food. A fed adult is **willing** for one game day. Two willing adults of the same kind that are close together walk to each other, and a young one appears. Both parents then rest for a day and need feeding again before they can breed again.

| Animal | Food | Willing pair must be within | Young grow up in |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[mob:sheep]] | [[item:wheat_item]] | 10 blocks | 1 day |
| [[mob:cow]] | [[item:wheat_item]] | 10 blocks | 3 days |
| [[mob:pig]] | [[item:carrot]], [[item:potato]] or [[item:beetroot]] | 10 blocks | 3 days |
| [[mob:chicken]] | [[item:wheat_seeds]] | 8 blocks | 2 days |
| [[mob:horse]] | [[item:wheat_item]] or [[item:hay_bale]] | 10 blocks | 1 day |

An animal that is not hungry refuses more food. Creative mode does not use up the food.

## Young animals

Lambs, calves, piglets, chicks and foals are half-size or so, with oversized heads. They drop nothing when killed, cannot be fed to breed, and cannot be sheared or milked. Feeding a young animal speeds its growing a little (about a tenth of the way to adulthood for each feed).

## Crowding

Animals will not breed when too many of their kind already stand close by: 30 sheep or chickens, or 24 cows or pigs, within 16 blocks. Wild breeding has an even stricter limit; see [[page:mob-spawning]].

## Following food

Cows, pigs and chickens within 10 blocks of a player holding their food walk after the player. Use this to lead a wild animal into a fence of your own. Sheep do not follow food.

## Products

- **Wool:** shears on a woolly sheep give 1 to 3 [[item:white_wool|wool]]. The wool grows back after about a week.
- **Milk:** an empty bucket on an adult cow gives a milk bucket, once per cow per game day.
- **Eggs:** adult chickens lay an egg every quarter to half a game day.

## Pens, coops, pastures and sties

Villages keep their animals in fenced buildings: a **pen** for sheep, a **coop** for chickens, a **pasture** for cows, a **sty** for pigs and a **paddock** for horses. Animals inside stay inside, and each building has a size limit. A keeper cares for them:

- The [[villager:shepherd]] feeds, shears and, when the pen is full, culls sheep.
- The [[villager:poultry_keeper]] collects eggs and culls past eight chickens.
- The [[villager:cowherd]] milks, feeds and culls.
- The [[villager:pig_farmer]] feeds, culls and cooks pork.
- The [[villager:stable_hand]] tames and breeds horses.

When their enclosure holds fewer than two animals, a keeper goes out and leads a wild one home. They never cull below two adults. Villagers cannot create animals from nothing: all their stock comes from the wild or from breeding. See [[page:village-structures]] for the buildings.
