The poultry keeper looks after the chickens of the village's coop. Its workstation is the [[item:nesting_box]], next to the coop. It is not a vanilla trade.

### Where poultry keepers come from
A village gets a poultry keeper for each of its chicken paddocks, with a coop beside the nesting box. Whether a village has one, and how many, comes from its paddock roll: bigger villages get more paddocks, and the animals native to the biome are likelier ([[page:village-structures]]). A village may have none, or several. The coop can stand on any ground and starts with 2 to 4 chickens. If a village has none, a [[villager:builder]] may put up a coop, if at least 2 wild chickens live within 80 blocks. Worlds made with village generator 9 or earlier keep the old rule: a poultry keeper in every village of 8 or more villagers.

### The working day
During working hours the keeper:

- **Collects eggs** from the coop's nest once it holds 3, and before the end of its day. Every adult chicken lays an egg every 5 to 10 minutes. A nest holds 64 eggs before new ones are lost.
- **Feeds** hungry chickens with wheat seeds so that they breed (see [[page:animal-husbandry]]). Chicks grow up in 2 days.
- **Culls** the flock once it passes 8 chickens, never below 2 adults. Each culled chicken gives raw chicken and 0 to 2 feathers, which the keeper keeps to sell.
- **Stocks the coop** if it holds fewer than 2 chickens, by fetching a wild chicken from within 100 blocks and leading it home, once a day at most.

A keeper with no coop tends the free chickens within 10 blocks of its nesting box.

### Where it gets its materials
It buys wheat seeds from the farmers when it runs low, keeping 4 back to lure a wild chicken home.

### What it sells and buys
Eggs, feathers and raw chicken. It buys wheat seeds from you. The [[villager:butcher]] buys its raw chicken and cooks it, the [[villager:baker]] its eggs and the [[villager:fletcher]] its feathers. Any villager who can cook buys eggs from it as cheap food.

### Starting kit
A founding keeper starts with 12 wheat seeds for feed. One who takes the trade later gets the emeralds for them. It never starts with eggs, feathers or raw chicken. Nothing is restocked.
