Crops grow only on farmland, and farmland needs water. This page covers how to prepare land, how crops grow and what speeds them up.

## Farmland

Right click grass, dirt or a dirt path with a hoe to turn it into farmland. There must be air above the block. Each tilling costs the hoe one use.

Farmland comes in two states, which look different:

- **Hydrated** farmland (dark) has water within 3 blocks horizontally, including diagonals, at the same level or one block above, or has rain falling on it with open sky overhead. Snow does not count.
- **Dry** farmland (paler) has had neither water nor rain for a while.

Hydrated farmland that goes without water or rain for a day may dry out, and is certain to by the third day. Dry farmland becomes hydrated again as soon as water or rain reaches it. Dry farmland with no crop on it turns back into dirt, at a random moment between one and fourteen days after it dried. A crop growing on it keeps it as farmland. A chunk that was unloaded during rain catches up when it loads again.

A ditch or a single water block therefore keeps a plot of about 7 by 7 blocks moist.

## Planting

Seeds, carrots and potatoes are planted with a right click on the top of farmland, hydrated or not. They cannot be planted on dirt.

- **Wheat:** from [[item:wheat_seeds]].
- **Carrots and potatoes:** plant the [[item:carrot]] or [[item:potato]] itself.
- **Beetroot:** from beetroot seeds.
- **Pumpkins:** from pumpkin seeds; the stem turns into a pumpkin where it stands.

## Growth

A young crop turns into a mature crop at a random moment. On average this takes about **four minutes** (a fifth of a game day), but any one plant may take much less or more. Growth does not need light. Crops only grow in loaded chunks, and wait while their chunk is away. Fast-forward speeds it up (see [[page:time-and-weather]]).

| Condition | Growth speed |
|---|---|
| Hydrated farmland | normal |
| Dry farmland | one third |
| While raining | 1.5 times |
| Within 8 blocks of a bee nest or hive with bees, in daylight | 1.25 times |

These stack. Harvest by breaking the crop; fully grown crops give food, seeds or both, and a young crop gives its seed back. Villagers farm in the same way: [[villager:farmer]]s till, plant and harvest.

### Sugar cane

Sugar cane grows up to three blocks tall when it stands on grass, dirt, sand or similar ground next to water. It is slow: each step takes about as long as a crop, and rain speeds it up. Only cane that you have planted or cut back grows; wild cane stays as it was generated until you cut it.

## Saplings and trees

A sapling planted on soil with room above turns into a full tree after about **eight minutes** on average, a little faster in rain. If something blocks the tree's shape, the sapling stays as it is. Leaves drop saplings, sticks and apples as they are broken. The [[villager:forester]] plants saplings and fells whole trees; chopping any log of a tree yourself also brings down the logs and leaves above it.

## Bees

Bees pollinate nearby crops: see [[mob:bee]]. A hive within 8 blocks gives the 25% boost listed above, only during the day and only while it has bees.
