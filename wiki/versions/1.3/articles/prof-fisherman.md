The fisherman cooks fish and sells it. Its workstation is the [[item:barrel]]. It has no other work than trading and visiting its barrel.

### The working day
Each day it cooks up to 8 raw cod that it holds into cooked cod, one for one.

### What it deals in
- It sells raw cod and, from the second level, cooked cod, in bigger batches as it levels up.
- It buys string, coal, sticks and feathers from you, and sells lanterns at the third level.

### Where its stock comes from
The game has no fishing, so the fisherman's cod is what it started with and nothing refills it. The daily restock leaves it alone. A fisherman who has sold its last cod has no fish left to sell.

Everything else about its day is common to all villagers: see [[page:villager-life]].
