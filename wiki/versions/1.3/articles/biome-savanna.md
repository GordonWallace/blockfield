Savanna is warm, dry grassland dotted with flat-topped trees. It lies between desert and plains in the hot-to-warm part of the climate map, and it also appears on dry foothills in hot country. There is no rain or snow.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] with patches of [[item:coarse_dirt]].
- The trees are acacias: a [[item:acacia_log]] trunk that bends partway up and ends in a flat, wide canopy of leaves. Some have a second, smaller branch going the other way. They are sparse.
- Short grass is plentiful. Flowers are very rare, and pumpkin patches sometimes appear.

### Villages
Savanna villages have a style of their own:
- Walls of [[item:acacia_planks]] with [[item:acacia_log]] corner posts.
- Roofs of [[item:orange_terracotta]].
- [[item:cobblestone]] foundations and wells, and grass between the buildings.
