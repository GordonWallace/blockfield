Sheep graze in open land, woodland and, in cold biomes, snow. Snowy zones hold nothing but sheep, so they are the animals you are likely to find in the far north. Wild groups are four to eight sheep. They are harmless, wander slowly, and run for a few seconds if you hit them. All sheep are white.

### Shearing

Right click an adult sheep with [[item:shears]] to take one to three wool without hurting it. The sheep then looks shorn and its wool grows back after about a week, a little sooner or later for each sheep. A shorn sheep drops no wool if killed, and lambs cannot be sheared. Wool is also a trade good in villages.

### Feeding and breeding

Right click with [[item:wheat_item]] to feed a sheep. A fed adult is willing for one game day, and a sheep that has just been fed refuses more. Two willing sheep within 10 blocks of each other walk together, and a lamb appears. The parents rest for a day before they can breed again. Lambs are small, drop nothing, cannot breed or be sheared, and become adults after one game day; wheat given to a lamb speeds its growth a little. Breeding stops when 30 sheep are within 16 blocks. Sheep, unlike cows and pigs, do not follow a player holding food. See [[page:animal-husbandry]] for the rules all animals share.

### Villages

Villages with a [[villager:shepherd]] have a fenced **pen** with a gate and a loom outside. A new pen starts with two to four sheep, and is full once it holds one sheep per six square blocks of its floor (six for the normal pen). The shepherd works through the day:

- feeds every hungry adult sheep, using wheat it bought from farmers;
- shears every woolly adult;
- culls sheep when the pen is full (shorn ones first), always keeping at least two adults, and keeps the mutton and wool;
- cooks its mutton and sells it to hungry villagers, and spins surplus wool into string at its loom, which fletchers buy for bows.

Sheep that wander near a pen join it. Without a pen, a shepherd tends the sheep within 10 blocks of its loom and counts eight as a full flock.
