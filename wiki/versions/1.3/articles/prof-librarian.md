The librarian trades in paper, books and light. Its workstation is the [[item:lectern]]. Every library building in a village has a librarian living in it, and the trade is also in the pool for ordinary houses.

A librarian does no work of its own beyond visiting its lectern. It trades with you and with the other villagers from its stock.

### What it deals in
- It buys paper and feathers from you, and later sells paper back. Villagers who craft maps buy their paper from it.
- It sells [[item:book|books]] and buys them at a better price, and at higher levels sells lanterns and buys them back.

### Where its stock comes from
The librarian is one of the few professions whose stock is still topped up by the daily restock: paper and books come back by about a quarter of their cap each day, up to the cap. Everything else it sells, such as lanterns or feathers, is what it started with or bought from you or from another villager.

### Starting kit
Between half and all of the most it can hold of each ware it sells, a small chance of some of the goods it buys, and 6 to 24 emeralds. Like every villager, it also starts with a few days of food.
