[[mob:villager|Villagers]] live in [[page:villages]]. Each one has a name, a bed, a purse of emeralds, an 18-slot inventory and, usually, a profession. They walk a daily routine, buy and sell with each other as well as with you, eat, breed and sleep. Hurting one makes the village's [[mob:iron_golem|iron golems]] angry with you for a minute.

## Appearance
A villager's robe, apron, belt and hat show its profession, and some carry a tool in hand (miners a pickaxe, builders a hammer). Nitwits wear a plain green robe. A villager with no job wears the plain robe of its biome. Children are about half size and run around.

Villagers walk slowly. [[villager:merchant|Merchants]] and [[villager:explorer|explorers]] walk one and a half times as fast. A villager flees from a zombie that comes within 8 blocks.

## Who lives in a village
Every bed in a village holds one villager (a few big houses hold two). The jobs are handed out when the village is first generated:

- The library always has a [[villager:librarian]], the church a [[villager:cleric]], and each smithy an [[villager:armorer]], [[villager:weaponsmith]] or [[villager:toolsmith]].
- Everyone else draws from the thirteen classic trades, least-used first so the same job is not repeated while others are missing. About one in ten is a [[villager:nitwit]].
- In the default world every village also gets a [[villager:miner]], a [[villager:farmer]], a [[villager:forester]] and a toolsmith, so that stone, food, wood and tools never run out.
- Other jobs depend on the village's size and ground, and are listed on each profession's page: [[villager:builder]], [[villager:merchant]], [[villager:explorer]], [[villager:furniture_maker]], [[villager:poultry_keeper]], [[villager:cowherd]], [[villager:baker]], [[villager:glassmaker]], [[villager:beekeeper]] and [[villager:pig_farmer]]. The [[villager:shepherd]], [[villager:poultry_keeper]], [[villager:cowherd]], [[villager:pig_farmer]] and [[villager:stable_hand]] come from a village's paddock roll (a village may have none, or several of a kind, though only one stable hand). In older worlds the stable hand never comes with a new village: it appears after a builder puts up a stable.
- A villager whose job is lost, a grown child with no free workstation and anyone who never had a workstation is [[villager:unemployed]] until it finds one. See [[page:villager-life]].

## The professions
- **Food and farming:** [[villager:farmer]], [[villager:butcher]], [[villager:fisherman]], [[villager:shepherd]], [[villager:poultry_keeper]], [[villager:cowherd]], [[villager:pig_farmer]], [[villager:baker]], [[villager:beekeeper]]
- **Wood, stone and building:** [[villager:forester]], [[villager:miner]], [[villager:mason]], [[villager:builder]], [[villager:glassmaker]], [[villager:furniture_maker]]
- **Smiths and crafts:** [[villager:toolsmith]], [[villager:armorer]], [[villager:weaponsmith]], [[villager:fletcher]], [[villager:leatherworker]]
- **Knowledge and travel:** [[villager:librarian]], [[villager:cleric]], [[villager:cartographer]], [[villager:explorer]], [[villager:merchant]], [[villager:stable_hand]]
- **No trade:** [[villager:nitwit]], [[villager:unemployed]]

Most professions do real work: they grow, make or dig what they sell, and buy what they lack from the others. Only a few things are still topped up by a daily restock, and each profession page says which.

## Levels
Every villager has five levels: Novice, Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert and Master. A villager earns experience from each trade it completes, whether with you or with another villager: 2 points for a Novice offer, then 5, 10, 15 and 30 for the higher levels. It reaches Apprentice at 10 points, Journeyman at 70, Expert at 150 and Master at 250. Each new level adds more offers to its trade screen.

## Trading
Open a villager's trade screen to see its offers, its level and a line saying what it is doing. The prices and offers of each profession are listed on its page. The rules are the same for everyone:

- A villager can only sell what it actually holds, and it needs room in its inventory for what you pay with. Otherwise the offer shows "Out of stock" or "Villager has no room".
- Each ware has a stock cap, so a villager buys only so much of one thing from you.
- A villager keeps what its next jobs need and will not sell it ("Keeping for own use").
- Its emeralds come from a small daily allowance (2 a day up to 12; builders 4 up to 80, explorers 6 up to 100, stable hands 3 up to 40) and from trading. With none left it cannot buy ("Out of emeralds").
- A villager that has gone more than a day without food only trades food.
- Prices drift with shortage and surplus: see [[page:village-economy]].

## Iron golems
A village is guarded by iron golems: about one for every 15 villagers, and at least one. Each patrols around a house. When a hostile mob attacks a villager, a golem within 64 blocks sprints to its rescue. Hurting a villager or a golem angers the village's golems at you for 60 seconds. A golem that is killed is not replaced.
