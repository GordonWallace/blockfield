Bees are small flying insects that live in nests and hives. They are not mobs you can attack: they have no health, and swords and arrows pass through them. They are drawn in large numbers at once without costing much, and at most 600 are out in the world at a time, those nearest the player first.

### Where they live

Each **bee nest** (found on about one in thirty oak and birch trees in forests and plains, in worlds made with the current generator) and each **beehive** holds up to 30 bees and a store of honey. A new nest starts with four to ten bees. Villages of six or more villagers on grass keep two hives with eight to twelve bees each, tended by a [[villager:beekeeper]].

### Daily life

Bees come out in the morning and head for flowers (poppies, dandelions and cornflowers) within 22 blocks of home. They land on a flower for a few seconds and fly back; they sometimes rest on the ground, leaves or walls along the way. Bees fly straight, a little above the tallest thing around them. A bee under leaves or a roof (a wild nest hangs just under its tree's leaves) first flies out from under them before it climbs, and a bee that meets an obstacle goes over it, along it or under it, whichever way is open. They return home half an hour before sunset and stay in all night, and also whenever it rains. No flowers, no honey.

### Honey and growth

Every visit to a flower followed by a return home adds one portion of honey. A home is full at five. A home with at least six flowers within range gains one bee for every game day it stays loaded, up to 30.

### Crops

Crops within 8 blocks of a loaded home with bees grow a quarter faster during the day; see [[page:farming]].

### Harvesting and stings

A full hive gives 3 honeycomb with shears or a honey bottle with a glass bottle. Harvesting it by day, or breaking the home, angers the bees that are out: each one stings you once for half a heart, then stops. The anger lasts 20 seconds. Bees never sting villagers. Break a home and its bees move into another home with room within 22 blocks, or vanish at dusk. A nest drops nothing; a broken hive drops itself.

### Leading

A bee follows any player or beekeeper holding a flower within 8 blocks (up to ten at once). Lead them within about two blocks of another home with room and one bee moves in.

### Bees and villagers

The beekeeper picks and replants flowers, harvests at dawn and dusk, builds new hives, leads bees from wild nests within 64 blocks to its hives, and sells honey, honeycomb and candles.
