The miner digs stone and ore out of the ground for the village. Its workstation is the [[item:mining_bench]], placed in or beside a house. Almost every new village has one, and every village in the default world does. See [[page:caves-and-ores]] for where ores lie.

### The working day
**Surface stone first.** The miner looks within 40 blocks of the village for stone above ground: a column whose top block is stone, coal or iron ore and stands one or two blocks above a neighbour. Digging these off levels outcrops and hillsides into walkable steps, and never leaves a pit. It never digs within 4 blocks of anything built or more than 12 blocks below the plaza. It walks to the spot and digs one block at a time.

**The mineshaft.** When no surface stone is in range, or once it has levelled up enough to go deeper, it digs a mineshaft just outside the village: a staircase 1 wide and 3 high going down, then a 2-high corridor straight on with a side branch 8 blocks long on each side every 4 blocks, and a torch every 6 cells while its torches last. It also digs ore it can harvest in the walls and ceiling of a cell it opens, but never ore next to water, and a cell that would open into water, a void or a building ends that shaft or branch. It leaves the shaft before the end of the working day, and starts a new shaft in another direction when one is finished.

How deep it goes depends on its level, because ores lie at depth: a Novice's staircase goes 20 blocks down (coal, iron, copper), an Apprentice's 40 (gold) and a Journeyman's or better 60 (diamonds, redstone). Below Novice depth it needs an iron or better pickaxe. When it levels up it abandons a shaft for a deeper one.

**Flint.** While a [[villager:fletcher]] in the village is short of flint, the miner also digs gravel, in its shaft and on the surface, and sifts it by placing and breaking it again until it drops flint, as you would.

### What it keeps
Cobblestone, coal, raw iron, raw gold, raw copper, diamonds, redstone, lapis, emeralds and flint. Dirt, plain gravel and other blocks it digs through are left behind. A miner with a full pack and goods no one buys stores them in a chest in its house, ordering one from the [[villager:furniture_maker]] if needed.

### Tools
The pickaxe wears out like yours, one use per block. A villager digs about two and a half times slower than you do with the same pickaxe. The miner never makes tools: it buys a new pickaxe from the [[villager:toolsmith]].

### What it sells
Cobblestone first, for the [[villager:builder|builders]]' foundations, then coal and raw iron, raw gold at Apprentice level and diamonds at Journeyman. Holding 32 cobblestone, it walks to a builder that needs some and sells it. A Novice stops digging at 256 cobblestone; a deeper digger carries on for ore and leaves extra stone behind. The [[villager:toolsmith]] buys its iron and the furniture maker its cobblestone for furnaces.

### Starting kit
A founding miner starts with a stone pickaxe and 30 to 40 torches. A villager who takes up mining later is given torches and emeralds for a pickaxe instead. It never starts with cobblestone, coal, ore or flint: it digs them. Nothing is restocked.
