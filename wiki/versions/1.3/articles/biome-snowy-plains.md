Snowy Plains are cold, open and white: a flat, treeless plain under permanent snow, in the coldest and driest part of the map. It borders snowy taiga, snowy beaches and frozen oceans. Snow falls here instead of rain.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:snow_grass]]: grass with a snow cover.
- Very few trees grow, only single [[item:spruce_log|spruce]] trees far apart.
- No flowers, grass or mushrooms grow.

In the weirder parts of the cold country the plains give way to [[biome:ice-spikes|Ice Spikes]].

### Villages
Snowy villages are built of spruce:
- Walls of [[item:spruce_planks]], [[item:spruce_log]] corner posts and roofs of oak planks.
- [[item:cobblestone]] foundations, and [[item:snow_grass]] between the buildings.
- Farms grow more potatoes than other crops.
