The butcher turns raw meat into cooked meat and sells both. Its workstation is the [[item:smoker]]. It has no other work than trading and visiting its smoker.

### The working day
Each day the butcher cooks up to 8 raw meats that it holds into their cooked form, one for one, and puts them on sale: [[item:cooked_chicken]], cooked porkchops, [[item:steak]] and cooked mutton.

### Where it gets its materials
- It buys raw chicken from the [[villager:poultry_keeper]], and raw porkchops from the [[villager:pig_farmer]], while it holds fewer than 9 of either chicken or pork, raw or cooked.
- It buys raw chicken, porkchops, beef and mutton, leather and coal from you.
- Leather it picks up from you is passed on as spare goods, which the [[villager:leatherworker]] buys.

### What it sells
Cooked chicken, porkchops, steak and mutton, at larger batches as it levels up.

### Restock and numbers
The daily restock does not touch the butcher: cooked meat is never made from nothing, only from raw meat it holds. Its stock of meat grows only when the poultry keeper and pig farmer sell it raw meat, or you do.
