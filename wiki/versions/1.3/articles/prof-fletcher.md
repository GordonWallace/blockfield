The fletcher makes arrows and bows. Its workstation is the [[item:fletching_table]].

### The working day
At its fletching table it crafts:

- **Arrows**, 4 at a time from a flint, a stick and a feather, taking half a game hour. It makes arrows while it holds fewer than 48.
- **Bows**, from 3 sticks and 3 string, taking a game hour. It makes them while it holds fewer than 2.

It makes whichever is further below its limit first, and a missing ingredient simply means it makes the other item. A craft it cannot finish before the day ends is finished on a later visit. When short of sticks it cuts them from planks.

### Where it gets its materials
The fletcher buys everything from the other villagers, at their prices, a batch for 16 arrows at a time:

- flint from the [[villager:miner]], who digs it out of gravel;
- sticks or planks from the [[villager:forester]];
- feathers from the [[villager:poultry_keeper]];
- string from the [[villager:shepherd]], who spins it from wool.

It buys whatever it can get even if another ingredient is not sold in the village.

### What it sells and buys
It sells arrows and bows from the first level, only as many as it has made. It buys sticks, flint, feathers, string and planks from you.

### Starting kit
A fletcher starts with no arrows or bows, just a few sticks, flint and feathers and some emeralds. Nothing is restocked: all its arrows come from its own work.
