The shepherd looks after the sheep of the village's pen. Its workstation is the [[item:loom]], which is placed beside the pen, outside the fence. Every shepherd's village is given a fenced pen for its flock.

### The working day
A shepherd walks between its pen and its loom:

- **Feeds** hungry sheep wheat, so that adult sheep in the pen will breed (see [[page:animal-husbandry]]).
- **Shears** woolly adults with its shears, which takes 1 to 3 wool. A shorn sheep grows its wool back after about 7 days, give or take a third.
- **Culls** adults when the pen is full. A pen counts as full at one sheep for every 6 cells of floor, and the shepherd never kills below 2 adults. It cooks the mutton, up to 8 a day, and sells it.
- **Spins** string at its loom: 1 white wool becomes 2 string, taking a quarter of a game hour per wool, up to 4 wool at a sitting. It only spins wool above the 16 it keeps for selling, and while it holds fewer than 16 string. It is a recipe only the shepherd uses; you cannot craft string from wool.

A shepherd with no pen tends the free sheep within 10 blocks of its loom.

### Where it gets its materials
It buys wheat for its flock from the farmers, a few at a time, as soon as it runs low. Its shears wear out, and when they do it buys a pair from the [[villager:toolsmith]].

### What it sells and buys
It sells white wool and string (the fletcher's bowstrings), buys wool from you, and at higher levels sells mutton. The [[villager:furniture_maker]] buys its wool for beds.

### Starting kit
A founding shepherd starts with a pair of shears and 8 wheat. A villager who takes up the trade later is given emeralds for shears and wheat instead. It never starts with string, because string is spun from its wool.

The daily restock leaves it alone: wool comes only from shearing.
