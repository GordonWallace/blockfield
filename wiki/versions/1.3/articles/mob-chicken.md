Chickens are small, quick birds that pace around in groups of four to eight. They are common in plains and forest, and mostly harmless. A chicken falls slowly instead of dropping, so it takes no fall damage, and it floats on water. If you hit one it runs away for a few seconds.

### Eggs

An adult chicken lays an egg every quarter to half of a game day. A wild chicken drops the egg where it stands; a coop chicken lays into the coop's nesting box. Eggs can be eaten once cooked in a furnace, and they are an ingredient in some foods.

### Feeding and breeding

Right click a chicken holding [[item:wheat_seeds]] to feed it. Chickens within 10 blocks of a player holding seeds follow them, so seeds are the way to lead chickens into a run. A fed chicken is willing to breed for one game day. Two willing chickens within 8 blocks walk to each other and a chick hatches. Chicks are half size, drop nothing and need two game days to grow up. Parents rest for a day. Chickens do not breed when 30 are within 16 blocks. See [[page:animal-husbandry]].

### Villages

A village with a chicken paddock has a [[villager:poultry_keeper]] who looks after a **coop**, a 7 by 7 fenced run with a gate and hay bales, and a **nesting box** beside it. A generated coop starts with two to four chickens. A chicken that wanders into the run joins the flock, and the run takes in up to 24.

The keeper's day:

- collects eggs from the nesting box once it has three or more;
- feeds hungry hens with seeds while the flock is eight or fewer;
- culls adults when the flock is over eight, always keeping two, and keeps a raw chicken and a few feathers;
- walks out to a wild chicken within 100 blocks and leads it home when the coop holds fewer than two, holding the gate open for it.

Keepers sell eggs, feathers and chicken. The feathers go to [[villager:fletcher|fletchers]] for arrows, and villagers also cook eggs for food (see [[page:villager-life]]). Builders can raise a new coop for a village that has none, if at least 2 wild chickens live within 80 blocks.
