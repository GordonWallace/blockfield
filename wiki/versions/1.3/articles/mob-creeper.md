Creepers are the silent monster with no weapon but themselves. They do not burn in sunlight. About a quarter of monster spawns are creepers, found in the dark on the surface at night and in dark caves at any time of day (see [[page:mob-spawning]]).

### The fuse

A creeper that sees you within 16 blocks walks towards you, slower than you can walk. When it is within 3 blocks with a clear line, it stops and starts hissing, swelling and flashing white. After 1.5 seconds it explodes. Once lit, the fuse keeps burning while you are within 6 blocks, even if you step back a little. It only dies away if you get more than 6 blocks off or out of its sight. Creepers ignore iron golems.

### The explosion

An explosion tears out a rough sphere about three blocks in radius with ragged edges. It destroys every block inside **except** bedrock and water, and the destroyed blocks drop nothing. A creeper can cut a hole in a house wall or a village path.

The blast hurts anyone in reach:

- Damage to you falls away with distance and ends 7 blocks from the blast.
- Standing right next to it costs about nine of your ten hearts; at 3 blocks it costs about five and a half.
- Every other mob within 6 blocks is hurt too, villagers and animals included.

### Staying safe

- A hit knocks it back, and a sprinting hit knocks it back further. Hit, then step away before the fuse finishes.
- The fuse stops growing if you break line of sight or get beyond 6 blocks.
- Light up the ground around your base: creepers appear only in the dark (see [[page:light]]).
- Players in creative mode and players asleep in a tent are ignored.

A creeper killed by you drops gunpowder. One that explodes drops nothing.
