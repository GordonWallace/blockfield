The cartographer makes compasses and blank maps. Its workstation is the [[item:cartography_table]]. See [[page:maps-and-compasses]] for how maps work.

### The working day
Standing at its cartography table, the cartographer crafts from what it holds:

1. a **compass** from 4 iron ingots around a gold ingot;
2. a **blank map** from 8 paper around a compass;
3. when it has plenty of paper (24 or more) and two blank maps in hand, it wraps the smallest blank map in 8 more paper to make the next size up.

It only makes a map while it holds fewer than 2 blank maps, so the supply stays small.

### Where it gets its materials
When short of iron, gold or paper and holding emeralds, the cartographer walks to a villager who sells them, such as the [[villager:armorer]] for iron and gold or the [[villager:librarian]] for paper, and buys at that villager's price. Its paper also comes back by the daily restock, up to its cap. You can sell it iron, paper and sugar cane directly.

### What it sells
Blank maps in five sizes, from 4 emeralds for the smallest to 64 for the largest, with the bigger sizes unlocked at higher levels, and compasses. It also sells paper and lanterns, and buys iron, gold, lanterns, sugar cane, paper and compasses from you.

The [[villager:explorer]] buys its blank maps from the cartographer.

### Starting kit and numbers
A cartographer never starts with a compass or a map. It always starts with the ingredients of one compass (4 iron ingots and a gold ingot), and about half the time with 8 paper as well, enough for its first map.

Every village that has a cartographer has an explorer for it 70 percent of the time.
