The weaponsmith works at the [[item:grindstone]]. It lives in a smithy, shared with the [[villager:armorer]] and [[villager:toolsmith]] (one of the three at random), or in an ordinary house.

It has no work of its own beyond visiting its grindstone, and trades from its stock.

### What it deals in
- It buys coal and iron ingots, and gold ingots, and sells iron swords at the lowest level.
- It buys and sells diamonds, sells diamond swords at Expert level and trades a diamond sword for an iron one with extra emeralds at Master level.

Axes are made by the toolsmith, not the weaponsmith.

### Where its stock comes from
Nothing is restocked, so it only has the swords it started with. A sword it sells is gone until someone else sells it one.

### Starting kit
Between half and all of the most it can hold of each ware it sells, a chance of some of the goods it buys, 6 to 24 emeralds, and a few days of food.
