Mushroom Fields are rare, strange islands far out at sea, away from any continent. They are quiet islands of fungus where nothing else grows. Rain falls here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:mycelium]].
- The trees are giant mushrooms, red-capped ([[item:red_mushroom_block]]) or brown-capped ([[item:brown_mushroom_block]]) on a [[item:mushroom_stem]], sparse.
- [[item:red_mushroom]] and [[item:brown_mushroom]] grow on the ground. No grass, flowers or other plants do.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on mushroom islands.
