The mason works at the [[item:stonecutter]] and sells building stone. It does nothing of its own beyond visiting its stonecutter, and trades from restocked stock.

### What it deals in
- It sells bricks, brick blocks, stone, mossy cobblestone, terracotta in several colours, sandstone bricks and calcite, with more kinds at each level.
- It buys clay balls, cobblestone, stone, sandstone and calcite from you.

### Where its stock comes from
The mason is one of the few professions whose stock is still topped up by the daily restock. Each day it gains about a quarter of its cap in bricks, brick blocks, stone, sandstone bricks and six kinds of terracotta, up to the cap. Its other wares, mossy cobblestone and calcite, are not restocked.

### Starting kit
Between half and all of the most it can hold of each ware, a chance of some of the goods it buys, and 6 to 24 emeralds, plus a few days of food.
