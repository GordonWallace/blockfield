An unemployed villager has no workstation. It wears the plain robe of its biome and has no trades. Children grow into this state, a villager whose workstation was broken falls back to it, and so does a villager born into a village with more people than jobs.

### What it does
It looks every few seconds for the nearest free workstation within 48 blocks of the village centre, walks to it, and takes the job once it stands beside the block. A grown child prefers the professions of its parents. A villager that lost a job remembers it for 30 days: put a workstation of the same profession back and it takes it at once. See [[page:villager-life]].

Meanwhile it eats, sleeps and wanders like everyone else. An unemployed villager is one of the things that lowers a village's happiness.

### Trading
It has no offers. When it holds less than a day's food, it will buy food from you: while its trade screen is open it pays an emerald for food worth about 88 percent of one, until it has three days of food.

### Starting kit
A few scraps and 1 to 6 emeralds, like a nitwit. Nothing is restocked.
