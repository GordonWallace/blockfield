The forester plants and fells trees. Its workstation is the [[item:band_saw]]. The profession is a recreation of the villager-planter mod. See [[page:trees-and-plants]] for how saplings grow.

### The working day
The forester picks planting or felling at random, weighted 10 to 8.

- **Planting.** A forester holding a sapling picks a free spot within 16 blocks of itself: air over soil with room for a tree above, no other sapling within 4 blocks, no door within 10 blocks and no building or other structure within 8 blocks (dirt paths are fine). It walks there and plants. After a sapling is planted it grows into a tree in about 8 minutes on average.
- **Felling.** It picks a natural tree within 40 blocks, nearer ones favoured, comes within 3 blocks and chops. It takes as long as you would to break every log of the tree one at a time with the same tool, and the whole tree comes down at once, leaves included. Buildings are never touched: a log pile next to planks or stone is not a tree. If the day ends or someone interrupts it, it keeps its progress on that tree.
- **Picking up.** It collects saplings, emeralds, logs, sticks and apples within 16 blocks, and after felling stays until nothing it wants lies within 12 blocks of the stump.
- **Sawing.** Up to 8 logs a day become 4 planks each, keeping 2 logs of each kind.
- **Sticks.** It keeps up to 64 sticks, made 4 from 2 planks.

### Axes
It chops with the best axe it holds: diamond, iron, gold, stone, then wood. It looks for a villager selling a better axe than its own every 20 seconds or so, and buys it. An axe wears out as yours does, one use per log.

### What it sells and buys
It buys oak saplings from you for an emerald each, as long as it has emeralds. It sells sticks, planks of each kind of wood, and logs, only what it actually holds. Farmers (for bed edges), [[villager:furniture_maker|furniture makers]], [[villager:builder|builders]], the [[villager:toolsmith]] and the [[villager:fletcher]] buy its wood.

### Starting kit
A founding forester starts with a wooden axe. A villager who takes up forestry later gets the emeralds for one instead. It never starts with logs, planks or sticks; it harvests them. Nothing is restocked.

### Numbers
Foresters are in every village of the default world, and a second one in villages with 19 or more buildings 40 percent of the time. Players: chopping any log of a natural tree also brings down everything above it.
