Pigs are the most common wild animal in woodland. They wander slowly in groups of four to eight, stop often to stand about, and avoid walking off drops or into water. A pig you hit runs away from you for a few seconds. They are harmless and never attack.

### Finding pigs

Wild pigs are placed in groups when land first loads, and a stretch of forest or taiga is often "pig country" where most groups are pigs (see [[page:mob-spawning]]). A few also live on open plains. They breed on their own, slowly, if two adults meet on grass and the area is not crowded.

### Feeding and breeding

Hold a [[item:carrot]], [[item:potato]] or [[item:beetroot]] and right click a pig to feed it. Pigs within 10 blocks of a player holding any of these walk after them, so you can lead a pig anywhere. A fed adult is willing to breed for one game day; two willing pigs close together walk to each other and a piglet appears. Piglets are half size, drop nothing and take three game days to grow up. Both parents then rest a day. Pigs do not breed when 24 or more are within 16 blocks. More on this in [[page:animal-husbandry]].

### Villages

A village with a pig paddock has a [[villager:pig_farmer]] and a **sty**, a small fenced yard with a gate, a water trough, a muddy corner and a roof over one corner. The yard holds three pigs at a time and starts with two to four. The pig farmer:

- feeds the pigs, and keeps a couple of carrots aside to use as a lure;
- culls adults when the sty is over its limit, always leaving at least two adults, and keeps the pork;
- leads a wild pig home from up to 100 blocks away when the sty holds fewer than two;
- cooks surplus raw pork in a village furnace, using fuel it buys, and sells it.

A pig farmer only creates pork from pigs it actually owns, and the [[villager:butcher]] buys its raw porkchops. Pigs are native to open land and woods, so villages there are likelier to have a sty. Builders can also add a sty to a village that lacks one, if at least 2 wild pigs live within 80 blocks; see [[page:builders]].
