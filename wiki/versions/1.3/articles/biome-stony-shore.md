Stony Shore is the rugged coast: bare rock, shingle and cliffs where the land meets the sea or where a mountain foot reaches the water. It replaces beach where the ground is steep and rough, so it often borders ocean on one side and hills or mountain slopes on the other. It occurs in every climate.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:stone]] with patches of [[item:gravel]].
- No trees, grass, flowers or mushrooms grow here, so the shore stays grey and bare.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on stony shores.
