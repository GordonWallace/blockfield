The armorer works at the [[item:blast_furnace]]. Every smithy has one of three smiths living in it, an armorer, a [[villager:weaponsmith]] or a [[villager:toolsmith]], chosen at random. The trade is also in the pool for ordinary houses.

The armorer has no work of its own beyond visiting its blast furnace. There is no armour in the game, so it deals in metal.

### What it deals in
- It buys coal and iron ingots from you and sells iron ingots too.
- At higher levels it sells and buys lanterns, diamonds and gold ingots, and sells larger batches of iron.

[[villager:cartographer|Cartographers]] and the [[villager:toolsmith]] buy their iron from it when they can.

### Where its stock comes from
Nothing is restocked. The armorer sells only the ingots it holds, which are its starting stock plus whatever you or another villager sold it. Once it runs out of iron it stays out until someone sells it more.

### Starting kit
Between half and all of the most it can hold of each ware it sells, a chance of some of the goods it buys, 6 to 24 emeralds, and a few days of food.
