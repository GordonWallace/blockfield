Desert is hot, dry country of open sand and dunes, found in the hottest and driest part of the climate map. It borders savannas, badlands and, toward the coast, beaches. There is no rain and no snow here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface and the first blocks below are [[item:sand]], over a layer of [[item:sandstone]].
- No trees grow. Tall, thin [[item:cactus]] stands here and there, one to three blocks high.
- [[item:dead_bush]] is scattered about. Nothing else grows, though [[item:sugar_cane]] grows at the edge of water.

### Villages
Desert villages have their own style:
- Walls of [[item:sandstone]], corner posts of [[item:sandstone_bricks]], and flat roofs of sandstone bricks instead of gables.
- Sandstone foundations and wells, and sand between the buildings.
- Dead bushes stand in the plaza's flower beds instead of poppies, and a garden with a few oak trees is added for the forester, because sand grows no saplings.
