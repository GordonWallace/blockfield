As a mob, a villager is a creature with 20 health that walks, opens doors and flees when hurt. It does not fight back. It is slow, so zombies catch it easily, and an [[mob:iron_golem]] will come running when one is attacked. Hurting a villager angers every golem of its village for a minute.

Villagers are never wild: they belong to the village that generated them or to the village they were born into. They are not stored or removed with chunks as wild mobs are; a village's villagers load with the village. You can summon one with the `/summon` command and give it a profession; see [[page:commands]].
