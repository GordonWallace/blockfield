Snowy Slopes are the cold flanks of the mountains: grassy snow low down, and bare snow and rock higher up. They occur in the frozen and cold climate bands, between the taiga foothills and the jagged peaks above. Snow falls here.

### Ground and plants
- On gentle ground the surface is [[item:snow_grass]]; higher up the ground is covered in [[item:snow]] blocks.
- On steep cliffs the rock shows through as bare [[item:stone]].
- No trees or other plants grow.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on snowy slopes, and sites near them are avoided.
