The nitwit is a villager who never works. It wears a plain green robe and has no workstation, no trades and no job to take. About one ordinary resident in ten is a nitwit.

A nitwit still lives like everyone else: it has a bed, eats, sleeps, wanders, and can have children. Its trade screen is empty. If it is hungry, it behaves like any villager and walks to buy food from the others.

### Starting kit
Two or three kinds of scrap (bread, bones, wheat seeds, sticks, apples or rotten flesh), 2 to 6 of each, and 1 to 6 emeralds. Nothing is restocked.

A nitwit never takes a workstation, even if you place one next to it; a [[villager:unemployed|jobless villager]] would. See [[page:villager-life]].
