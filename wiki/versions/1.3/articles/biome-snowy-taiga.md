Snowy Taiga is cold conifer forest under snow, in the frozen band where the air is not as dry as in the snowy plains. It borders snowy plains and snowy beaches, and it also covers the lower foothills of frozen mountains. Snow falls here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:snow_grass]].
- The trees are [[item:spruce_log|spruce]], moderately dense.
- Because the ground is snow-covered, no ferns, grass or flowers grow here.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in snowy taiga. The first village near the spawn point is the only exception: if it lands here it uses the snowy village style (see [[biome:snowy-plains]]).
