The beekeeper keeps bees and sells honey. Its workstation is the honey extractor ([[item:honey_extractor]]). It is not a vanilla trade. See [[mob:bee]] and [[page:farming]].

### Where beekeepers come from
In the default world, a village on grass with 6 or more villagers gets one, with an extractor, 2 beehives and a flower bed placed beside the farms. A builder may put up an apiary where there is none.

### The working day
The beekeeper looks after the beehives within 24 blocks of its extractor, up to 4. During working hours it:

- **Harvests** its full hives at dawn or dusk, alternating between shears (3 honeycomb) and a glass bottle (1 honey bottle) so that it has both.
- **Picks wild flowers** (never in reach of its own hives) and replants them around any hive with fewer than 6 flowers in reach.
- **Builds hives**: with 3 honeycomb and 6 planks (bought at market) it makes a beehive at its extractor and puts it by the farms.
- **Leads wild bees home.** With a flower in hand it leads bees from wild nests within 64 blocks into a hive with fewer than 10 bees.
- **Makes candles** from honeycomb and string at its extractor, until it holds 16.

### Where it gets its materials
Honey and honeycomb come only from full hives, flowers only from picking, hives from honeycomb and planks, candles from honeycomb and string. It buys planks, string and empty bottles from the other villagers. It keeps honeycomb for its next hive and bottles for its next harvest.

### What it sells
Honey bottles, honeycomb and candles. It buys empty glass bottles from you. Honey is a last-resort food for a hungry villager.

### Starting kit
A pair of shears and 16 glass bottles, once. It never starts with honey, honeycomb or candles. Nothing is restocked.
