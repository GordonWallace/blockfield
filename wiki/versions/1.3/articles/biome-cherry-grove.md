Cherry Grove is a mountain-foot grove of flowering cherry trees, found on the foothills of massifs where the terrain is unusual, taking the place of the meadow. It borders meadows and forests. Rain falls here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- The trees are [[item:cherry_log|cherry]], with a bent trunk and a broad, rounded blossom crown of [[item:cherry_leaves]].
- Short grass is plentiful. A few flowers grow.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in cherry groves. In worlds made with village generator 9 or earlier, the first village near the spawn point is the only exception when no better site is available.
