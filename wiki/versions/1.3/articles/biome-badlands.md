Badlands are hot, dry, weirdly shaped country: terraced mesas and ridges striped in red and orange, rising out of the desert. They occur only where the climate is hot and dry and the terrain is unusual, so they come in patches among deserts and savannas. There is no rain or snow.

### Ground and plants
- The flat tops are [[item:red_sand]]. On steep faces the sand is stripped away and the bare layers show, in bands of [[item:terracotta]]: plain, orange, yellow, white, brown and red. The bands run level through the hills.
- No trees. Occasional [[item:cactus]] and scattered [[item:dead_bush]] are the only plants.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in badlands, and sites near them are avoided.
