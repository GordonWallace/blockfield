Every block has a **hardness** and, often, a tool it is best broken with. How long a block takes depends on the tool's speed, whether you can harvest the block with it, and whether you are on solid ground.

## Tool types

- **Pickaxe:** stone, ores, bricks and most other mineral blocks.
- **Axe:** logs, planks, pumpkins and other wooden blocks. It can also strip the bark from a log to make a stripped log.
- **Shovel:** dirt, sand, gravel and similar loose ground.
- **Hoe:** tills grass, dirt and dirt paths into farmland (see [[page:farming]]).
- **Sword:** the best weapon; it also cuts pumpkins and melons a little faster.
- **[[item:shears]]:** shear sheep, clip wool and break leaves much faster.
- **Bow:** a ranged weapon; see [[page:player]].

## Tiers

Wooden, stone, iron and diamond tools make up four tiers, and golden tools are a special case. A higher tier mines faster and can harvest harder blocks.

| Tool | Tier | Speed | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wooden | 1 | 2 | 59 |
| Stone | 2 | 4 | 131 |
| Iron | 3 | 6 | 250 |
| Golden | 3 | 7 | 800 |
| Diamond | 4 | 8 | 1561 |

Golden tools are not as in Minecraft: they sit between iron and diamond, harvest like iron and last 800 uses. Shears last 238 uses and a bow 384.

Each tool type has a page comparing its materials, with mining times on common blocks: [[tool:pickaxe]], [[tool:axe]], [[tool:shovel]], [[tool:hoe]] and [[tool:sword]].

## What drops

Some blocks (stone, ores, obsidian and similar) give nothing if you break them with the wrong tool or too low a tier. They still break, just slowly. The tier a block needs:

- **Any pickaxe:** stone, cobblestone, bricks, sandstone, granite, coal ore and most other rock.
- **Stone pickaxe or better:** iron ore, copper ore, lapis lazuli.
- **Iron pickaxe or better:** diamond, gold, emerald and redstone ore.
- **Diamond pickaxe:** obsidian.

Swords, axes and shovels also have tiers but only matter for breaking blocks they are made for.

## Mining speed

The time to break a block is roughly the block's hardness times 1.5 (with the right tool tier, so it drops) or 5 (without), divided by the tool's speed, rounded up to the next twentieth of a second. By hand a block of dirt takes under a second; stone with a bare hand takes about 7 seconds and drops nothing; with a wooden pickaxe about 1.2 seconds, with iron about 0.4 and diamond 0.3. Obsidian takes about 9 seconds with a diamond pickaxe.

Two things slow you down: having your head under water, and not standing on the ground (jumping or swimming). Each makes mining five times slower, and both together twenty-five times. In creative mode every block breaks instantly.

## Durability

Each tool is worn down as you use it and breaks when it runs out. A broken tool is lost with a clink and a "Your tool broke" message. A tool's remaining life shows as a coloured bar on its slot.

- Breaking a block costs a tool one use, a sword two. Blocks with no hardness (grass, flowers, torches, crops) cost nothing.
- Hitting a mob costs a sword one use and any other tool two.
- Tilling with a hoe, stripping a log, shearing a sheep and drawing a bow each cost one use.
- Creative mode never wears anything.

Wear stays with the tool wherever it goes: in chests, thrown on the ground and in a villager's trade slot.

## Villagers' tools

Villagers' tools wear out by the same rules. A [[villager:miner]]'s pickaxe, a [[villager:farmer]]'s hoe, a [[villager:shepherd]]'s shears and a [[villager:forester]]'s axe all lose uses as they work, and a broken tool leaves the villager's pack and shows up in the village log. A villager whose tool broke has to buy a replacement; see [[page:village-economy]].
