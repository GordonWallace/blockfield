Sparse Jungle is the edge of the jungle: tropical woodland that lets the sun through. It lies between the dry savanna and plains on one side and the dense jungle on the other, in warm, humid country. Rain falls here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- Trees are a mix of tall [[item:jungle_log|jungle trees]], smaller jungle trees and ordinary [[item:oak_log|oak]], well spaced.
- Grass and ferns are common, and a few flowers appear. Melon patches ([[item:melon]]) grow on the grass in places.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in sparse jungle. The first village near the spawn point may be built there only if no better site is available.
