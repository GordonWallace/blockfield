The baker makes cakes and pies. Its workstation is the baker's oven ([[item:bakers_oven]]), which bakes like a furnace. It is not a vanilla trade.

### Where bakers come from
In the default world, a village of 15 or more villagers gets one baker for every 15 villagers, each with an oven placed by the village's plan. Where there is none, a [[villager:builder]] may build a bakehouse.

### The working day
The baker buys its ingredients and fuel, then:

1. turns sugar cane into sugar;
2. loads its oven with the ingredients of up to 2 cakes or 4 pies, adds fuel, and waits beside it;
3. with fuel burning, bakes a cake every 20 seconds of game time and a pie every 10;
4. takes out the food and any fuel left, and cuts each cake into 7 slices.

It bakes cakes while it holds fewer than 21 slices' worth, and pies while it holds fewer than 9. A **cake** is 3 milk bottles, 2 sugar, an egg and 3 wheat, and the empty bottles come back with it. A **pumpkin pie** is a pumpkin, sugar and an egg.

### Where it gets its materials
Everything is bought from other villagers: wheat and sugar cane and pumpkins from the [[villager:farmer|farmers]] (who grow cane and pumpkins for it), eggs from the [[villager:poultry_keeper]], milk bottles from the [[villager:cowherd]], and coal, charcoal, logs or planks as fuel. The empty bottles are its spare goods, which the cowherd buys back.

### What it sells
Cake slices, pumpkin pies and, from the second level, whole cakes. It buys eggs, wheat and pumpkins from you.

### Treats and happiness
Villagers buy a cake slice or pie as a treat at most every 3 days, when they hold none and have 2 emeralds, and eat at most one a day. A treat eaten in the last 3 days adds to the village's happiness ([[page:villager-life]]).

Nothing is restocked and the baker never starts with baked goods.
