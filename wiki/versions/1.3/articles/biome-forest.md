Forest is dense temperate woodland, with a thick roof of leaves and light patches of grass underneath. It lies between plains and the wetter birch and dark forests, and it grows on the lower foothills of the mountain ranges as well. Rain falls here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- The trees are mostly [[item:oak_log|oak]] (about two in three) with [[item:birch_log|birch]] among them. A few carry a bee nest ([[item:bee_nest]]).
- Short grass is common, in patches. Ferns, flowers ([[item:poppy]], [[item:dandelion]], [[item:cornflower]]) and mushrooms ([[item:red_mushroom]], [[item:brown_mushroom]]) are rare. Pumpkin patches may appear in clearings.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in forests. The only exception is the first village near the spawn point, which can be built in one if the land around the start has no better site.

Where the terrain is "weird" a forest turns into [[biome:flower-forest|Flower Forest]].
