Jungle is hot, humid forest at its thickest. Giant trees and a tangle of undergrowth roof over the ground, with ferns and melons below. It occurs in the wettest part of the warm and hot climate bands, bordering sparse jungle, savanna and the swamps of the coast. Rain falls here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- The trees are all [[item:jungle_log|jungle]]: huge trees with 2x2 trunks and wide crowns, medium trees with a single trunk and a few branches, and small ones. Low bushes of jungle logs and leaves fill the gaps.
- Ferns and grass are common, with a few flowers. [[item:melon]] patches grow on the grass.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in jungles, and sites near them are avoided.
