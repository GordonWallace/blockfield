The pig farmer looks after the pigs of the village's sty. Its workstation is the feed trough ([[item:feed_trough]]), beside the sty gate. It is not a vanilla trade.

### Where pig farmers come from
A village on grass with 10 or more villagers has a pig farmer 60 percent of the time, with a sty beside its trough. The sty has a gate, a coarse-dirt corner, a water trough and a small roofed shelter, and starts with 2 to 4 pigs. Where there is none, a [[villager:builder]] may build a sty, which starts empty.

### The working day
During working hours the pig farmer:

- **Feeds** hungry adult pigs a carrot, potato or beetroot, so they will breed, while the herd is at or under its limit of one pig per 8 cells of sty (see [[page:animal-husbandry]]). It keeps 2 back to lure a wild pig home. Piglets take 3 days to grow up.
- **Culls** adults while the herd is over the limit, never below 2 adults. Each yields 1 to 3 raw porkchops.
- **Stocks the sty**: if it holds fewer than 2 pigs it fetches a wild pig from within 96 blocks, once a day at most.
- **Cooks pork**: it keeps 6 raw porkchops to sell and cooks the rest in a furnace, with fuel it bought, until it holds 24 cooked.

### Where it gets its materials
Feed comes from the farmers, bought as it runs low. Fuel is bought too. Nothing is free: the pork comes from the pigs it culls.

### What it sells
Raw and cooked porkchops. The [[villager:butcher]] buys the raw porkchops. It buys carrots, potatoes and beetroot from you.

### Starting kit
6 to 10 carrots and 4 to 8 potatoes, to feed the sty. It never starts with pork. Nothing is restocked.
