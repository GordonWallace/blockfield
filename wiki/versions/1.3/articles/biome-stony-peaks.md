Stony Peaks are the barren summits of the mountains in warm and hot country. Unlike Jagged Peaks they are never snow-covered, so the rock stays bare. They rise from the slopes above hot savannas and deserts.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:stone]], with streaks of white [[item:calcite]].
- No trees or other plants grow.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on peaks.
