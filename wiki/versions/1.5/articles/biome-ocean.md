Ocean is open sea: the water that separates the continents, and the floor of every bay and gulf along their coasts. It occurs wherever the land falls below sea level far from the shore, so it is the usual neighbour of beaches, stony shores and, at river mouths, rivers. Out at sea, a few islands of mushroom fields can rise from it.

The sea has several variants named by the climate above it: **Frozen**, **Cold**, **Lukewarm** and **Warm** Ocean, with plain Ocean in the temperate band. Where the floor sinks far below the surface it is called **Deep** (Deep Cold Ocean, Deep Warm Ocean and so on). The variants differ in the floor they have, in how the water is tinted (cold seas darker blue, warm ones turquoise) and in ice.

### Ground and plants
- Shallow floors are [[item:sand]], with patches of [[item:clay]].
- Where the water is more than about five blocks deep the floor is sand in the warm seas (Lukewarm and Warm) and [[item:gravel]] in all the others.
- In Frozen Ocean the surface freezes to [[item:ice]] near the shore and in broken floes further out; the open water between the floes stays liquid.
- Nothing grows on the floor, and there are no trees.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in the ocean.

### On the planet
On the planet, about 60% of the surface is ocean. Its arctic seas are the separate Frozen Ocean biome.
