You get around the world on foot, by boat, or on horseback. All three can be mixed with the map and compass (see [[page:maps-and-compasses]]).

## Boats

Every kind of wood has its own boat, made from five planks of that wood. Boats burn as fuel too.

- **Launch:** right click water with a boat in hand and it is set down facing the way you look. You can also place it on land, where it only slides at a crawl.
- **Board:** right click the boat. W and S row forward and back (back is slower), A and D turn the boat and your view with it. Shift gets you out.
- **Speed:** about 8 blocks a second on water, up to about 40 on ice, and a slide of under half a block a second on land.
- **Rivers:** boats drift with flowing water and drop down river steps, but cannot climb them going upstream; get out and carry it.
- **Obstacles:** a boat never climbs a ledge or crosses a block. It never enters a chunk that is not loaded yet.
- **Passengers:** one mob can ride along with you. A mob that walks into the boat gets in; hitting the boat, or you getting out, lets it out. Iron golems do not ride, and neither do the animals of a village's pens, coops, pastures and sties.
- **Breaking:** three hits break an empty boat into its item. In creative mode one hit does, without a drop.

Boats are saved with the world. If you quit while sitting in one, you are in it when you return. Empty boats stay where you left them, even in unloaded areas.

## Horses

Wild horses can be tamed by riding them until they stop throwing you, and then saddled. A ridden horse is much faster than walking, jumps higher than you and carries your view with it. The full story is on [[mob:horse]]; the key facts:

- A **saddle** (leather over an iron ingot) is needed to ride a tamed horse.
- W and S move, A and D sidestep, and Space held charges a jump. Shift dismounts.
- The horse's health replaces your hunger bar while you ride. Falls hurt the horse, not you.
- A **lead** (string and leather) walks a horse beside you. Right click a fence to tie it.
- A village may keep a stable, whether generated with it or built later where wild horses live. A stable hand sells tamed horses (8 to 24 emeralds), saddles and leads, and buys horses you bring.

## Flying

You can also fly by double-tapping Space, in survival at about 11 blocks a second, or 22 sprinting, and 16 and 32 in creative. While flying, hold W and R together for a boost of ten times the normal flying speed. See [[page:player]].

## Villagers and travel

Villagers walk, but merchants and explorers walk half as fast again as other villagers, and merchants make trips between villages; see [[page:caravans]].
