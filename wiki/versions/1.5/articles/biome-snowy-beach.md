Snowy Beach is the beach of the frozen climate: a low shore between an icy sea and the snowy country behind it. It sits where Beach would otherwise be, so its usual neighbours are Frozen Ocean and Snowy Plains or Snowy Taiga. Snow falls on it rather than rain.

### On the planet
On the planet, Snowy Beach lines arctic coasts, next to Frozen Ocean.

### Ground and plants
- Under the water and at the waterline the ground is [[item:sand]].
- Just above the waterline the surface turns to [[item:snow_grass]], grass with a snow cover.
- There are no trees, flowers or other plants. The sugar cane of ordinary beaches does not grow here, because it needs unfrozen climate.
- The nearby sea freezes: look for [[item:ice]] on the water close to shore.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on snowy beaches.
