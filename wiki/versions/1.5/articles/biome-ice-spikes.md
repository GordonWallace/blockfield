Ice Spikes is a frozen wasteland of snow and ice towers, found only in the coldest, driest country where the terrain is unusual. It is rare and appears in patches within Snowy Plains. Snow falls here.

### On the planet
On the planet, Ice Spikes are found in the dry arctic, among the snowy plains near the poles.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is a layer of [[item:snow]] blocks.
- The spikes are columns of [[item:packed_ice]]: mostly thin ones that taper to a point, with a few much taller and wider ones.
- No trees or plants of any kind grow.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in ice spikes.
