The cleric lives in the church and works at the [[item:brewing_stand]]. Each church has one, and the trade is also in the pool for ordinary houses.

A cleric has no work of its own beyond visiting its brewing stand. It trades from the stock it started with and the goods it buys from you.

### What it deals in
- It buys [[item:rotten_flesh]] and [[item:bone]] at the first level, then gold ingots and gunpowder.
- It sells lanterns, gold ingots and diamonds, and at Master level a bell.
- It buys lanterns and diamonds back.

### Where its stock comes from
Nothing is restocked. The cleric sells only what it holds, so gold, diamonds and bells never reappear unless you or another villager sell them to it.

### Starting kit
Between half and all of the most it can hold of each ware it sells, a chance of some of the goods it buys, and 6 to 24 emeralds, plus a few days of food.
