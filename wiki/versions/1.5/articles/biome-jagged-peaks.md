Jagged Peaks are the highest, sharpest summits, snow-capped crags above the slopes. They form the top of the mountain ranges in the frozen, cold and temperate bands. Snow falls here.

### On the planet
On the planet, Jagged Peaks top the highest ranges in every band. The air is so much colder up there that even tropical summits carry snow.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:snow]] blocks, with bare [[item:stone]] on steep faces and patches of [[item:packed_ice]] on the flatter ground.
- No trees or other plants grow.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on peaks.
