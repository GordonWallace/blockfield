The cowherd looks after the cows of the village's pasture. Its workstation is the milk churn ([[item:milk_churn]]), beside the pasture. It is not a vanilla trade.

### Where cowherds come from
A village gets a cowherd for each of its cow paddocks, with a pasture beside the churn. Whether a village has one, and how many, comes from its paddock roll: bigger villages get more paddocks, and the animals native to the biome are likelier ([[page:village-structures]]). A village may have none, or several. The pasture can stand on any ground and always has a grass floor. It starts with 2 to 4 cows. A builder may put up a pasture in a village that has none, if at least 2 wild cows live within 80 blocks; it starts empty. Worlds made with village generator 9 or earlier keep the old rule: a cowherd in every village on grass with 10 or more villagers.

### The working day
During working hours the cowherd:

- **Milks** every adult cow once a day, and bottles the milk at the churn: 1 bucket of milk and 3 glass bottles make 3 milk bottles, and the bucket comes back.
- **Feeds** wheat to hungry adults while the herd is at or under its limit (one cow for every 8 cells of pasture). It feeds only as many as keep the herd within one over the limit once the calves come, counting every wild adult cow within 12 blocks of the pasture as if it had already walked in, fed or not. Pasture cows do not breed while the herd is over its limit (a wild cow from farther away can still wander in through the gate); a fed pair waits until the cowherd has culled back to the limit.
- **Culls** adults when the herd is over the limit, never below 2 adults. Each yields raw beef and leather, kept to sell.
- **Stocks the pasture** if it holds fewer than 2 cows, by leading a wild cow home from within 100 blocks.
- **Cooks steak** from raw beef in a real furnace with fuel it has bought, keeping 6 raw beef to sell and cooking the rest until it holds 24 steaks.

Calves take 3 days to grow up and give no milk. See [[page:animal-husbandry]].

### Where it gets its materials
Wheat comes from the farmers. It buys empty glass bottles back from the village whenever it has fewer than 9, up to 30, from the villagers who drank the milk or the [[villager:glassmaker]] who makes new ones. Fuel for the furnace is bought as well.

### What it sells and buys
Milk bottles, raw beef, leather and steak. It keeps 3 milk bottles for the [[villager:baker]]. It buys wheat and empty bottles from you. It never sells its bucket.

### Starting kit
A bucket and 30 glass bottles, once per villager. It never starts with milk, beef, leather or steak. Nothing is restocked.
