Flower Forest is a lighter, brighter cousin of the forest: open woodland whose floor is a carpet of blossoms. It appears in the same temperate, middling-humid country as Forest, but only where the terrain is unusual, so it is found in smaller areas, surrounded by ordinary forest or plains. Rain falls here.

### On the planet
On the planet, Flower Forest is a temperate biome, with a few patches in the subtropics.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- Trees are thinner than in a normal forest: [[item:oak_log|oak]] with some [[item:birch_log|birch]], some with bee nests ([[item:bee_nest]]).
- Flowers are very common. They grow in large patches, and each patch is a single colour: [[item:poppy]], [[item:dandelion]] or [[item:cornflower]].
- Short grass is common between the patches. Pumpkins grow in rare clumps.

Flower patches draw [[mob:bee]] to the trees' nests, so this is the best place to look for them.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in flower forests.
