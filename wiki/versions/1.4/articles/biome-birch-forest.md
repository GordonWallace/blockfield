Birch Forest is a bright, tall forest of pale trunks, found in humid temperate country, between Forest and the dark forest of the wettest lowlands. Rain falls here.

### On the planet
On the planet, Birch Forest belongs to the humid temperate band, with a little in the subtropics.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- The trees are mostly [[item:birch_log|birch]], with a few [[item:oak_log|oak]] mixed in, and a small share carry bee nests ([[item:bee_nest]]).
- Short grass is common. Ferns and flowers are rare. Pumpkin patches sometimes appear.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in birch forests. In worlds made with village generator 9 or earlier, the first village near the spawn point is the only exception, when it has no better site.
