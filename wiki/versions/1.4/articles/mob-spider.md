Spiders are fast, wide and low, and the only monsters that climb. Spiders are **off by default**: they spawn only in worlds whose game options turn them on (see [[page:mob-spawning]]). Then about one monster spawn in six is a spider, found in the dark on the surface at night and in dark caves at any hour (see [[page:mob-spawning]]). Unlike zombies and skeletons they do not burn in sunlight.

### Neutral by day

In bright light a spider leaves you alone unless you attack it. It counts as bright when the light at its spot is above roughly 60% of full. Once you hurt it, it stays hostile for 30 seconds even in daylight. At night, in caves and in deep shade it hunts you like any other monster, noticing you within 16 blocks with a clear line of sight.

### Attack

A spider is the fastest monster, though still slower than you at a sprint. It runs at you and bites for 2 points of damage (a heart) once a second. From 2 to 4 blocks away it sometimes pounces, leaping towards you in an arc. It is wide, so it cannot squeeze through a one-block gap.

### Climbing

When a spider that is hunting or wandering runs into a wall, it climbs it. Fences, cliffs and house walls that stop zombies do not stop spiders, so a wall alone is not protection. A closed door is: like other monsters except zombies, spiders cannot get through one.

### Other fights

A spider that is not calm picks a fight with an [[mob:iron_golem]] within 10 blocks, doing 3 points a hit. The golem kills it in a couple of blows. Spiders do not hunt villagers.

Spiders drop string, which you need for bows and leads. Village shepherds spin string from wool too; see [[mob:sheep]].
