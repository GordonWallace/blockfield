Meadow is a high, open pasture of grass and flowers on the lower slopes of the mountain ranges, in temperate to warm country. It borders forests, cherry groves and the steeper hills above. Rain falls here.

### On the planet
On the planet, meadows cover dry temperate high plains (around y 900 and up), and the cooler high plains of the subtropics and tropics, as well as massif foothills.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- Trees are almost absent: just the occasional [[item:birch_log|birch]], some with bee nests ([[item:bee_nest]]).
- Short grass is plentiful. Flowers are very common, in big single-colour patches of [[item:poppy]], [[item:dandelion]] and [[item:cornflower]], so [[mob:bee]] are often seen.
- Pumpkin patches sometimes appear.

### Villages
Meadows use the plains village style: oak planks walls, [[item:oak_log]] corner posts, roofs of [[item:spruce_planks]] and [[item:cobblestone]] foundations. See [[page:villages]].
