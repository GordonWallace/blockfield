The toolsmith makes the village's tools. Its workstation is the [[item:smithing_table]]. Every village in the default world has one, and every smithy building houses an [[villager:armorer]], [[villager:weaponsmith]] or toolsmith at random.

### The working day
Standing at its smithing table, the toolsmith crafts one tool every 2 game hours (it finishes a tool on a later visit if the day ends first). It makes:

- pickaxes, axes and hoes in wood, stone, iron, gold and diamond;
- iron [[item:shears]] and [[item:bucket|buckets]].

It follows the usual recipes: 3 head items and 2 sticks for a pickaxe or axe, 2 and 2 for a hoe, 2 iron ingots for shears, 3 for a bucket. Planks of any wood, cobblestone, iron ingots, gold ingots and diamonds are the heads.

It keeps its stock even. It makes a tool while it holds fewer than 2 of that kind in that material or better, and never holds more than 4 of one kind. It keeps only one hoe, one pair of shears and one bucket. It always tries the best material it can get, but if the better material is sold in the village and simply out of stock (the miner is out of cobblestone, say), it waits up to 6 game hours before making a lesser tool, as long as it already has one of that kind on the shelf (or the lesser tool would only be wooden). Each wait shows on the debug screen's Economy view as something it wanted and could not buy, from the hour it begins, even when it begins at its smithing table just before the working day ends.

### Where it gets its materials
It makes everything from what it buys from the other villagers, at their prices:

- sticks and planks from the [[villager:forester]];
- cobblestone, coal and raw iron or raw gold from the [[villager:miner]], and iron, gold and diamonds from anyone who sells them;
- fuel for smelting: coal and charcoal first, then logs and planks.

With a furnace the toolsmith can use, it buys raw iron or gold and smelts it itself: it loads the ore, burns the fuel already in the furnace first, waits, then takes out the ingots and all the fuel left. If the village has no usable furnace, it buys one from the [[villager:furniture_maker]] and puts it down inside the house of its bed. Only one villager uses a furnace at a time.

### What it sells and buys
Every tool it makes is for sale from the first level, limited by what it holds. Higher levels add more materials that it buys from you: iron, sticks, planks, raw iron, gold and diamonds. It also buys coal and cobblestone from you. The village's farmers, foresters, miners and shepherds buy their tools from it.

### Starting kit
A toolsmith starts with no tools at all, because it makes them, only a few planks and sticks and some emeralds.
