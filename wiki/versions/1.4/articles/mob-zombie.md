Zombies are the commonest monster, making up a third of all spawns (they are on by default; a world's game options can turn them off, see [[page:mob-spawning]]). They appear in the dark on the surface at night and in dark caves at any hour (see [[page:mob-spawning]]). They are slow, but they never give up, and they are the main reason villagers shut themselves indoors at night.

### Hunting

A zombie notices you when you come within 16 blocks with a clear line of sight. It then walks straight at you, and keeps coming until you are more than 28 blocks away. It is slower than you even when you walk, so you can always outrun it. With no player to chase it wanders, but it also hunts villagers: any villager within 16 blocks becomes its target.

When a zombie reaches you or a villager it hits once a second for 3 points of damage (a heart and a half). Players in creative mode, and players asleep in a tent, are ignored.

### Breaking doors

A zombie that is chasing a player or a villager and stands pressed against a closed wooden door for three seconds breaks it down, both halves at once, and the door drops as an item. This works in survival only. No other monster can open or break a door. Fence gates are not doors: zombies do not break them.

### Burning

Zombies burn in daylight. Out in the open with the sky light at their head at 12 or more, they catch fire and lose one point of health a second, which kills an unhurt zombie in twenty seconds. They are safe:

- in water;
- under a roof or in deep shade;
- in rain or snow, and in thunderstorms;
- at night.

### Golems

A zombie that is not chasing you will pick a fight with an [[mob:iron_golem]] within 10 blocks, even leaving a villager for it, and hurts it for 4 points a hit. The golem hits back much harder. Golems sprint to rescue any villager under attack.

### Zombies and villages

Zombies wander into villages at night. A villager has 20 health, so a zombie needs about seven hits to kill one. No monster spawns within 12 blocks of a villager, but one that walks in from elsewhere will hunt the nearest villager it can reach. See [[page:villager-life]] for what villagers do at night, and [[page:villages]] for how villages are laid out.

A killed zombie may drop rotten flesh.
