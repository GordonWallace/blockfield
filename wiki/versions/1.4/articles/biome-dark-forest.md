Dark Forest is a gloomy, crowded wood in the wettest of the temperate lowlands. The canopy is so thick that little light reaches the floor, and mushrooms thrive in the shade. It borders birch forests and forests. Rain falls here.

### On the planet
On the planet, Dark Forest grows in the wettest parts of the temperate band.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] with scattered patches of [[item:podzol]].
- Most trees are [[item:dark_oak_log|dark oak]]: thick 2x2 trunks and a wide, flat canopy that meets its neighbours. Some are ordinary [[item:oak_log|oak]], and a few are giant mushrooms with red or brown caps ([[item:red_mushroom_block]], [[item:brown_mushroom_block]]) on a [[item:mushroom_stem]].
- [[item:red_mushroom]] and [[item:brown_mushroom]] are common on the floor. Grass is scattered. No flowers grow.



### Villages
Villages do not generate in dark forests, and sites near them are avoided.
