Horses are the fastest way to cross the land. They live on open land (plains, savanna and meadow), in wild groups of four to eight like the other animals; some stretches of open land are horse country (see [[page:mob-spawning]]). No two horses are alike: each rolls its own health, running speed and jump height, from a slow, low-jumping pony to a racer that clears walls over five blocks high, along with one of seven coats and five markings.

### Taming

Right click an untamed horse with an empty hand to climb on. It bucks and throws you off every few seconds, and each fall adds to its temper. The higher its temper, the better the chance that the next buck is its last: it stops throwing you and is tamed. You can speed this up by feeding it [[item:wheat_item]], an [[item:apple]], a [[item:hay_bale]] or [[item:sugar]]; hay raises temper most. Tamed horses are yours: they are saved, never despawn, and no one else can ride or lead them.

### Riding

A tamed horse needs a [[item:saddle]] before you can ride it; right click with one to fit it. On horseback:

- W and S move forward and back (back is slow), A and D step sideways, and the horse faces where you look;
- hold Space to charge a jump, and the longer you hold, the higher it jumps, up to its own limit;
- Shift gets you off; sneak and right click with an empty hand takes the saddle back.

Your hunger bar is replaced by the horse's health. Falls hurt the horse, not you. If the horse's head goes under water you are put off. See [[page:transport]].

### Leads and feeding

A [[item:lead]] used on a horse makes it follow within three blocks of you; the lead snaps if you get more than twelve blocks away. Right click a fence post to tie every horse you are leading to it. Food heals a tamed horse: hay heals most.

### Breeding

Feed a tamed adult wheat or a hay bale to make it willing for a game day. Two willing horses of one owner within 10 blocks find each other and a foal appears, with stats close to the average of the parents. A foal cannot be ridden or saddled and grows up in a day.

### In villages

A village near wild-horse land may be generated with a **stable**, and a builder may put one up where at least 2 wild horses live within 80 blocks. A stable is an open shelter with a tack rack and a fenced paddock. A generated stable starts with 2 tamed horses. Its [[villager:stable_hand]] catches wild horses within about 100 blocks by feeding them until they accept it, leads them to the paddock, breeds up to four, and sells them to the player for 8 to 24 emeralds, based on their stats. It also buys horses you lead to it.
