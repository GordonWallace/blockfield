Skeletons are archers. They make up a quarter of monster spawns, appearing in the dark on the surface at night and in dark caves at any time of day (see [[page:mob-spawning]]). They are slower than you on foot.

### Combat

A skeleton that sees you within 16 blocks starts shooting. It fires an arrow every 1.6 to 2.4 seconds, aiming at your chest and allowing for gravity, with a little random spread. An arrow that hits does 2 to 4 points of damage (one to two hearts). One that misses sticks in the ground and disappears after about three seconds. You cannot pick up skeleton arrows.

It also keeps its distance:

- Farther than about 11 blocks away, or without a clear line to you, it walks towards you.
- Closer than 5 blocks it backs away.
- In between it strafes slowly sideways, changing direction now and then.

A skeleton will not walk off a drop of more than three blocks. It ignores players in creative mode and players asleep in a tent. Like other monsters it cannot get through a closed door.

### Burning

Like zombies, skeletons burn in daylight where the sky light at their head is 12 or more, losing one point of health a second. Water, a roof or deep shade, rain, snow, thunderstorms and the night all protect them.

### Golems

A skeleton that is not chasing you will shoot at an [[mob:iron_golem]] within 10 blocks, doing 3 to 5 points an arrow. The golem will hunt it down in return.

### Fighting skeletons

Their strength is range, and their weakness is that they walk slowly. Close the distance sideways rather than head-on, or break their line of sight around a corner and fight there. You can also answer with a bow (see [[page:mining-and-tools]]). They drop bones and arrows, but only when you deliver the killing blow.
