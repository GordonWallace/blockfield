Beach is the sandy strip where land meets a calm sea. It lies right at the waterline, low and almost flat, between the ocean and whatever biome is inland. In frozen country the strip is replaced by Snowy Beach, and along rocky, rugged coasts by Stony Shore.

### On the planet
On the planet, beaches line coasts from the tropics to the subarctic. In the arctic they become Snowy Beach.

### Ground and plants
- The surface and the first few blocks below are [[item:sand]], over [[item:sandstone]].
- There are no trees and no grass, flowers or mushrooms.
- [[item:sugar_cane]] grows where the sand touches water at sea level, in clumps one to three blocks tall.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on beaches. In worlds made with village generator 9 or earlier, the first village (the one nearest the spawn point) can end up on one if no better site can be found near the start.
