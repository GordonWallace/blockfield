The stable hand catches, breeds and sells horses. Its workstation is the [[item:tack_rack]] in a stable.

### Where stable hands come from
A new village within 100 blocks of wild-horse land may be generated with a stable and its stable hand if the paddock roll draws a horse paddock. Whether a village has one, and how many, comes from its paddock roll: bigger villages get more paddocks, and the animals native to the biome are likelier ([[page:village-structures]]). Horses are native only to open land (plains, savanna and meadow), but any village can draw one. A generated stable starts with 2 tamed horses, given the first time its village runs, whether you are there or it runs in the background ([[page:village-loading]]), like the other paddocks' starting animals. A village has at most one stable, so at most one stable hand.

A [[villager:builder]] may also put up a stable in a village that has none, but only if at least 2 wild horses live within 80 blocks, and rarely. A villager with no job takes the stable's tack rack. Worlds made with village generator 9 or earlier never generate stable hands with a village; there one appears only after a builder puts up a stable.

### The working day
Its day, most urgent first:

1. Lead a horse it has caught home: open the gates, put it in the paddock, close the gates.
2. Fetch a paddock horse that is loose.
3. While the paddock holds fewer than 2 horses, catch the nearest wild horse within 100 blocks of the rack. It walks to it and feeds it wheat or hay from its own pack until the horse accepts it, then leads it home. With no wild horse in range it waits until the next morning.
4. With 2 or 3 horses, feed wheat or hay to a pair of ready adults so they breed. It keeps the paddock at 2 to 4 horses. It never lets foals take it past 4.
5. Otherwise shop for supplies.

At the rack it makes saddles (3 leather and an iron ingot) and leads (4 string and a leather make 2). Far from you, where horses do not exist as mobs, catching and breeding pause, but shopping goes on. See [[page:transport]] for horses.

### Where it gets its materials
It starts with no feed and no tack. It buys wheat, hay, leather, string and iron from the other villagers' offers, at their prices. Nothing is created: the saddles and leads only exist once made from those.

### What it sells and buys
- Leads from the first level, saddles from the second.
- Adult horses: one offer for each paddock horse beyond the 2 it keeps, priced from the horse's speed, jump and health at 8 to 24 emeralds. The horse walks out on your lead, if the stable hand has one to give.
- It buys wheat, hay, string and leather from you, and it buys horses: lead a tame adult horse of yours to the stable hand and use it, and it pays the price when it has the emeralds and room, and leads the horse home.

It has a bigger purse than most: it gets 3 emeralds a day up to 40. Nothing is restocked.
