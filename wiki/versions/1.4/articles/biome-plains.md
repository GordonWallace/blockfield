Plains are open, level grassland: the broad, gently rolling heart of the temperate lowlands, with few trees and a wide sky. Plains lie between forests and deserts and savannas. In the warm band they appear in drier country, and they are the biome where most villages stand. Rain falls here.

### Ground and plants
- The surface is [[item:grass]] over [[item:dirt]].
- A tree stands here and there: a lone [[item:oak_log|oak]], and a few of them carry a bee nest ([[item:bee_nest]]) tucked under the leaves, with [[mob:bee]] around it.
- Short grass is plentiful, in patches. Flowers are scattered: [[item:poppy]], [[item:dandelion]] and [[item:cornflower]] grow in small single-colour clumps.
- Pumpkin patches sometimes appear on the grass, and [[item:sugar_cane]] grows beside water.

### Villages
Plains are the home of the plains village style:
- Walls of oak planks, with [[item:oak_log]] corner posts and a gabled roof of [[item:spruce_planks]].
- [[item:cobblestone]] foundations and well, and grass between the buildings.
- A village of six or more people on grass also gets a beekeeper with beehives and a flower bed.
