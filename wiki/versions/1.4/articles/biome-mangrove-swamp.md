Mangrove Swamp is the warm version of the swamp: flooded, muddy lowland behind the tropical coast. It forms in the same flat, humid lowlands as the swamp but where the climate is warm, so it sits close to the sea and borders jungles and beaches. Rain falls.

### On the planet
On the planet, Mangrove Swamp lines low, wet tropical coasts.

### Ground and plants
- The ground, above and below the water, is [[item:mud]] to a depth of several blocks.
- The trees are mangroves: a [[item:mangrove_log]] trunk with a rounded top of [[item:mangrove_leaves]], held up by prop roots that arch down from the trunk into the mud. They grow densely and may stand in shallow water.
- Almost nothing else grows. Mud does not carry grass, flowers or mushrooms.

### Villages
Villages do not generate in mangrove swamps, and sites near them are avoided.
