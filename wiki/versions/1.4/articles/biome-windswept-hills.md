Windswept Hills are bare, steep hills on the mountain flanks in temperate country, between the forested foothills and the snowy peaks. Rain falls here, turning to snow high up.

### On the planet
On the planet, Windswept Hills sit on the upper slopes of ranges where the air is temperate at that height, which on the planet means mostly the tropics and subtropics.

### Ground and plants
- On gentle ground the surface is [[item:grass]]; on steeper ground and higher up it is bare [[item:stone]], with some [[item:gravel]] patches on slopes.
- A few [[item:spruce_log|spruce]] trees grow on the grass, scattered, and none grow on the highest ground.
- Grass is scattered, and flowers are very rare.

### Villages
Villages do not generate on windswept hills, and sites near them are avoided. In worlds made with village generator 9 or earlier, the first village near the spawn point may be built there if nothing better is available.
