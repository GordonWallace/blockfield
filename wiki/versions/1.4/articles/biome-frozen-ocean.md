Frozen Ocean is the sea of the planet's arctic: the cold water around the poles, mostly roofed with ice. It occurs wherever the arctic land falls below sea level away from the shore, so its usual neighbours are Snowy Beach, Snowy Plains and Snowy Taiga, and further from the poles the ordinary Ocean. Snow falls here. Flat worlds have no Frozen Ocean biome; their coldest seas are the Frozen variant of Ocean.

### On the planet
Frozen Ocean covers the arctic seas, beyond about 66 degrees from the equator, and reaches into the subarctic where the band edge wanders south. About 5% of the planet is Frozen Ocean.

### Ground and plants
- The surface freezes to [[item:ice]] near the shore and in broken floes further out; the open water between the floes stays liquid.
- Shallow floors are [[item:sand]] with patches of [[item:clay]]; where the water is more than about five blocks deep the floor is [[item:gravel]].
- Where the floor sinks below y -30 it is called **Deep Frozen Ocean**.
- Nothing grows.

### Villages
Villages don't generate at sea.
