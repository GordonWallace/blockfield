Release 1.4: everything in 1.3, on a round planet.

The planet
- Every new world is a round planet: a cube of six faces puffed out into a sphere, 655,360 blocks around the equator (#280, #281)
- The ground carries on across the edges between faces with no seam, and the eight corners sit out in open ocean (#281, #282, #284)
- Near the ground the world looks flat as before; fly up and it bends into a round planet, then space with stars (#284, #285)
- Fly high in creative with a boost that grows with height, and look down on the whole planet from orbit, dragging it round with the mouse (#285, #335, #336)
- Torches, water, grass, trees, animals, villagers, monsters, drops, boats, horses, bees, chests and furnaces all work across face edges (#283, #288, #290, #306, #309)

Land and climate
- The same terrain as 1.3's mile-high worlds, laid on the sphere: lowlands, escarpments, high plains, massifs, badlands, caves and ores (#282)
- Climate follows latitude in five bands from the tropics to the poles, with every 1.3 biome and a new Frozen Ocean (#287)
- Rivers worked out for the whole planet: every one runs down to the sea or into a lake, and lakes sit in hollows and highland basins (#298, #315, #325)

Seeing far
- Distant terrain out to about 65 km, with forests, seas and snow in their true colours (#293, #333)
- Trees and village houses drawn far into the distance, fading into the forest colour as they shrink (#320, #338)
- The whole planet drawn as a globe from orbit (#322)
- Render distance up to 32 chunks, the new default (#320)

Finding your way
- Coordinates as face, u, v and height, with latitude, longitude and true north on the debug screen (#292)
- A map of the whole planet (M), a teleport panel in creative (P), and commands that take latitudes, longitudes and corners (#292)
- Compasses, maps, signposts and explorers point the right way across faces (#294)
- Explorers sell a map only once it is fully drawn (#389)

Villages
- Villages on every face, in the same styles as 1.3, never cut by an edge (#291)
- Village loading, village life and caravans measured round the planet (#295, #296)
- Villagers at leisure no longer hold up a village's unload, and builders time their own pace (#391, #395)
- Villagers and golems no longer bounce under ledges (#398)
- Fixes found along the way: herd limits counting wild animals near pens, stables near wild horses, villagers and closed doors, villager buckets that take the water they fill from (#313, #347, #348, #349, #351, #358, #359, #361, #368, #378)

Debug screen
- A game log for each world, with a live viewer, filters, Copy and Download (#383)

Worlds
- Worlds made with 1.3 can't be opened in 1.4; they show greyed out with only Delete (#297, #356)
