The first numbered Blockfield release. It includes everything built before releases began: climate biomes, mile-high terrain, continents and rivers, caves and ores, about 200 vanilla blocks, villages with jobs and real inventories, farming, maps and compasses, foresters, furniture makers and shepherds, iron golems, fast-forward, saves, day and night, weather and sound.

New in 1.0:
- Rivers merge and widen (#36)
- Second-monitor debug screen, with a reliable feed and log filters (#41, #51, #52, #53, #57)
- Vanilla mining times and drops (#44)
- Miner villagers and tool durability (#43)
- Farm beds (#46)
- Wood variants: doors, gates and more in every wood (#45)
- Villager screens, statuses, claimed beds and the creative inventory (#40)
- Chest owners and villager storage (#47)
- Escape never opens the pause menu from a screen (#55)
- Builders build with any wood they acquired legitimately (#42)
- Auto map size cap, ported from 1.1 (#110)
- GitHub CI with baseline and integration tests (#54, #85)
