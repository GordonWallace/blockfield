Release 1.1: everything in 1.0, plus:

Gameplay
- Day and night fade gradually, and monsters arrive well after dark (#64)
- Held items look right: grass and leaves are tinted, flowers and torches are flat (#95)
- Mouse capture survives Escape from screens; Z frees the mouse; middle click and an F3 mouse readout (#108, #58, #65, #68, #74)
- Fences and closed gates can't be jumped (#149); torch light crosses chunk borders on tall builds (#138)
- Ore frequency changes with depth (#91)
- Farmland hydration (#96), a composter on every farm (#97)

Villages
- The tool economy: toolsmiths, tool wear and repair through trades, villagers holding and swinging their tools (#67, #69, #71, #72, #73, #78 to #83)
- Villagers climb ladders (#48, #60), only foresters fell trees (#93), villagers stay where you left them (#104)
- Many village fixes from the bug hunt: dead villagers' beds and workstations are freed, builders never take a held bed, children leave the house in the morning, paid-for chests stay reserved, and more

Fixes
- Over 40 fixes from the bug hunt (bug-001 to bug-044): dropped items and full inventories, saves and reloads, maps, beds and sleep, furnaces, commands (/tp, /time, /setblock, /give), signs, weather and world seeds
- Auto map size cap (#90, #92) and dropped items saved (#94)

Tools
- Debug screen additions (#70, #76, #77)
- Run scripts for Windows, Mac and Linux (#49)
