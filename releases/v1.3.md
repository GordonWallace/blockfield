Release 1.3: everything in 1.2, plus:

New villagers and animals
- Cowherds with pastures, milking, calves and milk bottles (#133)
- Bakers with ovens, cakes and pumpkin pies; place a cake and eat it slice by slice (#194)
- Pig farmers with sties and piglets; lead pigs with a carrot (#266)
- Poultry keepers with coops and chicks, and fletchers who make arrows (#124)
- Glassmakers with glass kilns and sand pits, and glass pane windows (#261)
- Bees and beekeepers: hives, honey, candles, and bees that follow a held flower (#265)
- Horses: tame, saddle and ride wild horses, and stables in horse villages (#117)

Getting around
- Boats you can craft and row (#114)
- Bows and arrows (#124)
- Signposts on main roads that point to nearby villages (#239)
- Builders bridge wide rivers (#263)

Trade and economy
- One market for players and villagers, with spare goods and needs on the trade screen (#125)
- Prices follow demand: shortages raise them and gluts lower them (#116)
- Merchants run trading caravans between villages up to 600 blocks apart (#129, #247, #251)

The living world
- Mobs live in the world: monsters spawn around you at night and far chunks keep their animals (#262)
- Sky light spreads naturally under overhangs, through doorways and under forest canopies (#273)
- Quit and reload takes no game time, so everything comes back exactly as it was (#245)
- Villagers return to a believable place after you've been away (#253)
- Villages finish their villagers' tasks before they unload, and unload early when the game gets heavy (#248, #251, #256, #258)

Debug screen
- Economy flow view, village comparison table, and a villager's day timeline (#113, #115, #180)
